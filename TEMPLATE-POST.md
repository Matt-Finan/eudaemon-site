Write the post here, in ordinary markdown. Paragraphs are just blank-line-separated text. **Bold**, *italic*, [a link](https://example.com).

## A section heading gets the gold dot automatically

Tables work as normal markdown tables and get the site styling for free.

When you change your mind about something written in an older entry, go to THAT entry, add this where the old claim sits, and bump the "revisions" count in its front matter:

::: revised 12.08.2026
What changed and why, linking to the entry that changed it: see [entry 05](/log/05-whatever/).
:::

Delete everything above the front matter's closing --- that you don't need; delete this instruction text too.
