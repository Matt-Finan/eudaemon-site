I'm Matt Finan, an AI security consultant; Eudaemon is a thing I'm building.

To summarise, the project: claim every record I can lawfully obtain about myself (about 70 sources so far, bank statements to heart-rate traces), hold it in one encrypted archive, and find out whether analysis over that archive can improve the extent to which my choices are informed; everything I know about myself brought to bear on a decision, rather than whatever I happen to remember. What it is now, in full, lives on [the blueprint](/blueprint/); the latest progress is in [the log](/).

Before serious resource goes in, I'm putting the idea itself under scrutiny; decomposing it into the beliefs it relies on (62 ranked tests), and testing against failure criteria written in advance. When I update my thinking, the change is dated and kept on the record.

Where it goes if the tests pass: a real build, and in time, I'd hope, a commercial one.
