New entries can come to you instead of you checking here. The site publishes a feed; any feed-reader app watches it and shows each entry the minute it's published.

The feed address is:

**`https://eudaemon.uk/feed.xml`**

Copy that address into a feed reader (Feedly, NetNewsWire, Reeder and plenty of others; most are free) and you're done. If you open the address directly in a browser you'll see a wall of code; that's normal, feeds are written for reader apps rather than for people.

No reader? The [log](/) always carries everything, newest first.
